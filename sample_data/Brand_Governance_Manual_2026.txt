BRANDGUARD AI - OFFICIAL BRAND GOVERNANCE MANUAL 2026
Organization: Innovexa Technologies Inc.
Classification: Approved Corporate Policy
Effective Date: January 2026

SECTION 1: BRAND TONE & VOICE
1. Rule 1: Brand voice must remain confident, empathetic, and professional. Avoid aggressive sales slang like 'dirt cheap', 'kill the competition', or 'crappy'. Speak with understated authority and genuine care for institutional trust.
2. Rule 2: In crisis communications or compliance alerts, use measured, transparent, and reassuring language. Never speculate or downplay security inquiries.

SECTION 2: TERMINOLOGY & PRODUCT NAMING
3. Rule 3: Always spell the product name as 'BrandGuard AI' with uppercase B, G, and AI. Never spell it as 'brandguard' or 'Brandguard'.
4. Rule 4: Always refer to our analysis technology as 'Grounded AI Governance' instead of 'generic AI' or 'black-box filter'.
5. Rule 5: Refer to human evaluators as 'Authorised Brand Reviewers' or 'Brand Managers', never as 'content censors'.

SECTION 3: CORE MESSAGING PILLARS
6. Rule 6: Our core value proposition statement is: 'Intelligent brand governance – catch inconsistencies before content is published.' Use this exact wording across public campaigns and sales enablement collateral.
7. Rule 7: Always emphasize human control: 'AI suggests corrections, but authorized humans make the final approval decision.'

SECTION 4: PROHIBITED CLAIMS & LEGAL COMPLIANCE
8. Rule 8: Never claim '100% bug-free' or make absolute guarantees such as 'zero risk guarantee'. All performance claims must be qualified with empirical benchmarks and standard disclaimers.
9. Rule 9: Do not promise instantaneous regulatory immunity or legal indemnification under any regional jurisdiction.

SECTION 5: APPROVED FACTS & INSTITUTIONAL METRICS
10. Rule 10: Approved institutional metric: BrandGuard AI is trusted by over 450+ enterprise compliance teams worldwide. Do not fabricate unverified customer figures or inflate metrics beyond audited reports.
11. Rule 11: Innovexa Technologies was established in 2024 with headquarters in Noida, India, specializing in explainable enterprise AI systems.

SECTION 6: EDITORIAL FORMATTING & STYLE
12. Rule 12: All social media and ad headlines must use Title Case capitalization and never terminate with double exclamation marks (!!).
13. Rule 13: Always apply the Oxford comma in serial enumerations within official product announcements.

SECTION 7: VISUAL IDENTITY & COLOR CODES
14. Rule 14: Approved primary brand colors are Deep Navy (#0F172A) and Electric Orange (#F97316). Background surfaces should remain clean white or soft slate (#F8FAFC).
15. Rule 15: Minimum clear space around the BrandGuard AI logo must equal the height of the capital letter 'B'.
